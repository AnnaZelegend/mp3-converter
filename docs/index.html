For Mac

Links in.
MP3s out.

Paste a YouTube or Spotify link, press Convert, and get an MP3 with the title, artist and cover art already filled in.

Install

  1. 1
    Get HomebrewIf you don't have it yet, follow the one-line instructions at brew.sh.
  2. 2
    Paste this into TerminalIt installs everything the converter needs and puts the app on your Desktop.
    curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AnnaZelegend/mp3-converter/main/install.sh | bash
  3. 3
    Double-click “MP3 Converter”The converter opens in your browser. Paste links, choose a folder, press Convert.

What it does

YouTube

Videos, playlists and YouTube Music links.

Spotify

Songs, albums and playlists, matched to the same song on YouTube Music.

Private

Runs entirely on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

For personal use. Downloading may go against YouTube's and Spotify's terms of service. Built on yt-dlp and spotDL.
